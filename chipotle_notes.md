## Key Risks
    - Supply chain may not be able to support long-term goals, delaying growth goals. Shortages or increases in cost of ingredients affects Chipotle financially.
    - Food safety: Chipotle historically has dealt with food poisoning outbreaks, exacerbated with ingredients being left at room temperature un-refrigerated. 
    - Increasing labor costs (minimum wages & employee benefits) leads to higher menu prices or absorbing inflation.
    - Heavy reliance on IT systems is risky as  interruptions/failures could lead to the loss of guests or employees.
