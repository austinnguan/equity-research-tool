## Key Risks
    5 Risks that seem underappreciated:
        - The battle for consumers and their screentime: Duolingo not only has to compete with other language tutor services, but the rise of LLM's also poses a threat to Duolingo. Paired with the fact that Duolingo is competing for screen time, Duolingo in that sense is also competing against social media apps & gaming apps, not just educational products.
     

    Profability may be hard to increase let alone maintain: 
        - New releases such as the Math/Music course or even the "Duolingo Max" subscription hasn't generated "material revenue". 

    Risks of being known as a mission-based company: 
        - Duolingo was built with a focus on keeping free language learning available worldwide, regardless of economic backgrounds. This makes it harder for them to generate profits (ex: subscriptions) without coming at the cost of their reputation. 

      


    Risks that are buried (on page 30):
    Uncertainty of International Markets: 
        - Duolingo's growth is reliant on emerging international markets. China in the future may prevent Duolingo from operating with political tensions. Regulations in China are heightened and lead to increased scrutiny, regulations, and compliance costs.

    Reliance on Data Collection: 
        - Data privacy laws are constantly changing and as a result may lead to an increase of operating costs. Data collection also comes with the risk of privacy violations, something that Duolingo should be cautious about.  

    Increased use & regulation of AI: 
        - Duolingo has incoporated ML and AI to help produce content for the learning part of the app and the English Test. Generative AI is subject to accuracy issues and biases, which could hurt Duolingo's reputation or relations with customers. Regulations on AI around the world are becoming more strict, with Duolingo alluding to the US, EU and China all coming out with new framework. 

    The crossroads of Intellectual Property & AI: 
        - The curent field of intellectual property protection in AI is constatnly changing, leaving question marks on whether or not Duolingo is able to protect its own AI systems. If not, competiors maay be able to take advantage of Duolingo's research, hurting Duolingo's business as a whole.  

## Moat
    Name Brand 
        - When thinking about online language tutors, Duolingo comes first to mind. Duolingo is known as the top language learning app, with it being the top choices on both Google & Apple app stores. 
        - Duolingo's light green bird is an icon on social media, with 17 million followers on TikTok. 
    Streaks:
        - Streaks are a helpful mechanism that keep users consistent and engaged. Streaks have helped Duolingo with user retention as they incentvize a specific amount of time allocated for Duolingo each day. 
    AI/ML Models:
        - Duolingo's AI/ML models have been extremely effective in keeping users engaged with accurate personalization
